Scan this code in the Lactic app

That code pairs an iPhone with Lactic on your computer. The Camera app can't use it: open Lactic on your iPhone and scan it from there.

  1. Open Lactic on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Pair with your computer, or go to Settings → Your computer.
  3. Tap Scan the code and point it at your computer's screen.

The pairing key in the code stays on your devices: it's after the # in the link, which browsers never send to a website.
