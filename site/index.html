Local first

Your data is yours.

Every ride, plan and night of sleep sits in one file on your Mac. No account to make, nothing to export when you leave. Back it up like any other file.

Bring your own AI

Your coach is your call.

Plug in any AI key or run a model on your own machine. Already pay for Claude or ChatGPT? Connect it through the built-in MCP server. Rather ride in peace? Turn the coach off.

Built-in coach

Any OpenAI-compatible key, Ollama or LM Studio. Keys stay in your Keychain.

Claude or ChatGPT

Lactic's MCP server gives them the same tools the built-in coach has.

No AI

The plan still builds and adapts. Nothing leaves your Mac.

The engine owns the math

The math is open.

Your plan, your zones and your fitness come from rules anyone can read. The AI explains and suggests. It never invents a number, and it never changes your plan without you.
