Lactic

Plans

A plan that fits your life.

Tell Lactic™ your goal and the hours you have. It builds the weeks, the workouts and the rest, shows where your fitness will land, and adapts as you go.

Rides

Every interval, on target.

ERG holds the watts, road mode frees you for sprints. Start pedaling and the workout starts.

  • Stays on top of other windows and training apps
  • Every interval is a lap on Strava and intervals.icu

Recovery

Train for the day you're having.

A score each morning from sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, against your own usual range. Your plan adjusts to it.

  • Apple Watch, Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, Polar, Coros or Suunto

Coach

A coach that knows your numbers.

Ask if you're overdoing it, or how to pace your race. It reads your plan, rides and recovery, and you apply its changes with a click.

  • Built in, with the AI you pick
  • Claude Desktop or any MCP app
  • No coach, just the plan

My legs feel heavy and Ridgeline is in 12 days. Am I overdoing it?

Download

Download Lactic

Lactic is in beta. Expect a few rough edges, and tell us what you find with Send feedback in the app.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Lactic to Applications.
  2. Open Lactic.

Not tested on Windows yet. If something doesn't work, tell us with Send feedback or on GitHub.

  1. Run the installer.
  2. Windows warns about an unknown publisher. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Lactic from the Start menu.

Not tested on Linux yet. If something doesn't work, tell us with Send feedback or on GitHub.

  1. Make it runnable: chmod +x Lactic_*.AppImage
  2. Run it.
