Counting…

By release

  • Counts come from GitHub, which tallies every fetch of each release file. They include repeat downloads, our own testing and the odd bot, so read them as a trend, not a head count.
  • Downloads adds up the Mac .dmg, Windows installer and both Linux files. The Mac .dmg and Linux .deb are only fetched by people installing. The Windows installer and AppImage are also fetched by the in-app updater.
  • Mac updates are in-app updates. Update checks count installed copies asking for news of a release. Preview builds are left out.
  • The iPhone app's installs are in App Store Connect under TestFlight.
